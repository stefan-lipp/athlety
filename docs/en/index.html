Your competition planner
for track and field.

Find competitions in your region, save your favorites, and keep track of the whole season — all in one app.

Features

Find competitions

Browse all track and field competitions in Germany with up-to-date data from the LADV.

Filter

Filter by regional association and discipline to find exactly the competitions that suit you.

Bookmark

Save competitions you're interested in as bookmarks — synced across all your devices via iCloud.

Calendar export

Export competitions straight to your calendar so you never miss a registration deadline.
